# Feeling worried about a result

## It is normal to worry

Many people feel scared after an abnormal screening result. Remember that screening is designed to find changes early, when they are easiest to treat, and most follow-up tests do not find cancer.

## Things that can help

- Write down your questions before the appointment.
- Bring someone you trust.
- Ask the care team to explain anything you do not understand, as many times as you need.

## When to get help

If worry stops you sleeping, eating or getting through the day, tell your doctor. If you feel unsafe or think about harming yourself, call 998 or 999 now.
