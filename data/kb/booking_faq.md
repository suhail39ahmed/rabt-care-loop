# Booking, rescheduling and cancelling

## How to book

Rabt suggests in-network clinics near you with the earliest slots. You can confirm a slot in the app or ask the care team to book for you.

## Changing or cancelling

You can reschedule or cancel from your case page. Please cancel at least 24 hours before so the slot can go to someone else. If you miss an appointment, Rabt will contact you to find a new time; your case stays open.

## What to bring

- Your Emirates ID and insurance card.
- Your referral letter, if you have one.
- A list of your medicines.

## Languages and preferences

You can ask for an interpreter, a female clinician, or an appointment outside working hours where available.
