# Cervical screening (HPV and smear) results

## What the test checks

Cervical screening checks for high-risk human papillomavirus (HPV) and, if needed, for changes in cervical cells. It is a prevention test, not a cancer test.

## Your result

- **HPV not found:** your risk is very low. You will be invited again at the routine interval.
- **HPV found, cells normal:** a repeat test is usually advised in about a year, to see if your body has cleared the virus, which is common.
- **HPV found with abnormal cells:** you will usually be referred for a colposcopy.

## What abnormal means

Abnormal cells are changes that could, over many years, develop into cancer if left untreated. Finding and treating them early is exactly what screening is for. An abnormal result is not a diagnosis of cancer.

## Colposcopy

A colposcopy is a closer look at the cervix with a magnifying lens. It takes about 15 to 20 minutes, and a small sample may be taken. You can ask for a female clinician.
