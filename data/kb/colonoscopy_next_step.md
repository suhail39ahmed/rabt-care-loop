# What happens after a positive FIT — approved FAQ

The usual next step after a positive FIT is a referral to a gastroenterology clinic, where a specialist decides whether a colonoscopy is needed.

A colonoscopy lets a doctor look inside the bowel with a thin camera. The clinic gives you written preparation instructions, including what to eat and drink the day before. Follow the clinic's instructions, not general advice from the internet.

You can ask for a female clinician, an Arabic-speaking clinician, or an interpreter when you book.
If you are worried, you can ask for a call from the care team before you book. This is not a diagnosis.
