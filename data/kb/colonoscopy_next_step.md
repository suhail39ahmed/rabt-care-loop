# What happens after a positive FIT

## The usual next step

The usual next step after a positive FIT is a referral to a gastroenterology clinic, where a specialist decides whether a colonoscopy is needed.

## What a colonoscopy is

A colonoscopy lets a doctor look inside the bowel with a thin, flexible camera. Most people have sedation and go home the same day. Small polyps can often be removed during the same test.

## Your choices

You can ask for a female clinician, an Arabic-speaking clinician, or an interpreter when you book. If you are worried, you can ask for a call from the care team before you book. This is not a diagnosis.
