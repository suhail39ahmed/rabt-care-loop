# Getting ready for a colonoscopy (bowel prep)

## Why preparation matters

The bowel must be empty so the doctor can see clearly. If it is not, the test may need to be repeated. Always follow the written instructions from your clinic; they may differ from this general guide.

## Days before

- A few days before, many clinics ask you to avoid seeds, nuts, whole grains and raw vegetables.
- Tell the clinic early if you take blood thinners, iron tablets, insulin or other diabetes medicines. Do not stop any medicine unless your clinician tells you to.

## The day before

- Clear fluids only (water, clear broth, apple juice, plain tea without milk).
- Drink the bowel preparation exactly as instructed, usually in two parts. Stay near a toilet.

## On the day

- Follow the fasting time your clinic gives you.
- Bring a list of your medicines and someone to take you home, because you should not drive after sedation.
