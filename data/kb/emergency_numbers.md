# Emergency numbers in the UAE

## Call now

- **998:** ambulance.
- **999:** police.
- **997:** civil defence (fire).

## When to call 998

Call an ambulance for chest pain, trouble breathing, heavy bleeding, fainting, a seizure, signs of a stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech) or severe pain after a procedure.

## Rabt is not an emergency service

Messages in Rabt are not monitored every minute. Do not wait for a reply in an emergency; call 998 or go to the nearest emergency department.
