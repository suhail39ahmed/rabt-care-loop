# Talking to a nurse or doctor (callback)

## When to ask for a callback

Ask for a callback when you want a person to explain your result, when the FAQ does not answer your question, or when you are unsure about your medicines or preparation.

## How it works

Tap "Talk to a person" on your case page. A coordinator reviews the request and, when it is a clinical question, a nurse or doctor calls you back. Rabt tracks the callback against a time limit, and it escalates to a supervisor if no one calls.

## What we cannot do in chat

We cannot tell you a diagnosis, change your medicines or interpret your personal result in chat. Those questions always go to a clinician.
