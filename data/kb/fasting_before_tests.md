# Fasting before tests and procedures

## Which tests need fasting

Some blood tests (such as fasting glucose or a lipid profile) and procedures with sedation need an empty stomach. HbA1c and FIT do not need fasting. Your booking message says if fasting is needed.

## How to fast safely

- Usually nothing to eat for 8 to 12 hours before a fasting blood test; plain water is normally allowed.
- For sedation, the clinic gives you a separate cut-off time for food and for clear fluids.
- If you have diabetes, ask your clinician how to adjust your medicines on a fasting day. Do not skip or change doses on your own.

## Fasting during Ramadan

If your appointment falls during Ramadan, tell the booking team. Many tests can be booked after iftar or rescheduled.
