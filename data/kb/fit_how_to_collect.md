# How to collect a FIT sample

## Before you start

Read the kit leaflet first. Write your name and the date on the tube label. You do not need to change your diet or stop medicines for FIT.

## Collecting the sample

1. Put the collection paper in the toilet, or use a clean, dry container, so the stool does not touch the water.
2. Open the tube and scrape the stick over the stool in a few places until the grooves are covered.
3. Put the stick back, click the cap shut and do not open it again.

## Returning the kit

Return the sample within the time printed on the kit (usually a day or two), using the bag provided. If you are on your period or have bleeding haemorrhoids, wait until it stops and ask the clinic if unsure.
