# Negative (normal) FIT result

## What a negative FIT means

A **negative** (normal) FIT means no hidden blood was found in the sample you sent. It is reassuring, but no screening test is perfect, and a normal result is not a guarantee.

## When to test again

Screening works best when it is repeated. Your programme will invite you again at its usual interval (often every one to two years). Keep your contact details up to date so the reminder reaches you.

## Symptoms do not wait for the next test

If you notice blood in your stool, a lasting change in bowel habit, unexplained weight loss or ongoing tummy pain, contact your doctor even if your last FIT was normal.
