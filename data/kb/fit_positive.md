# Positive FIT result: what it means

## What a positive FIT means

A faecal immunochemical test (FIT) looks for tiny amounts of hidden blood in stool. A **positive** (abnormal) FIT means blood was detected and **further checks may be recommended**.

A positive FIT does **not** by itself mean you have cancer or any other disease. This is not a diagnosis. Blood can come from many common, harmless causes such as haemorrhoids or a small tear.

## Why follow-up still matters

FIT cannot tell where the blood came from. The usual next step is a referral to a specialist, who may recommend a colonoscopy. Finding and removing small growths (polyps) early is one of the main goals of screening.
