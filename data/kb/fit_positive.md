# Positive FIT — approved FAQ

A faecal immunochemical test (FIT) looks for hidden blood in stool.

A **positive** FIT means blood was detected and **further checks may be recommended**.
It does **not** by itself mean you have cancer or any other disease.

Rabt will help you find an in-network next step and an owner for your case.
Always talk to a clinician for personal medical advice. This is not a diagnosis.
