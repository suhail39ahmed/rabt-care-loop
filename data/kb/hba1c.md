# HbA1c screening follow-up — approved FAQ

HbA1c is a blood marker used in metabolic screening programmes.

An HbA1c above the programme's follow-up threshold means your care team may
recommend a confirmatory test or a visit. **It is not a diagnosis of diabetes.**

Your Rabt case has an accountable owner and will close only with attendance
evidence or a documented human decision. Ask to talk to a person any time.
