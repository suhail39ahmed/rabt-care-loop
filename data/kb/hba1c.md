# HbA1c screening follow-up

## What HbA1c measures

HbA1c is a blood marker used in metabolic screening programmes. It reflects average blood sugar over about the last two to three months. No fasting is needed.

## A result above the follow-up threshold

An HbA1c above the programme's follow-up threshold means your care team may recommend a confirmatory test or a visit. **It is not a diagnosis of diabetes.**
