# Demo Health Insurance: if a claim is rejected

## Read the reason first

Every rejection should give a reason, such as a missing document, a service not covered, or no pre-approval.

## How to appeal

1. Ask the clinic for any missing document or a corrected invoice.
2. Send an appeal to Demo Health Insurance within the time stated in the rejection letter, with a short explanation and the documents.
3. Keep the reference number. Rabt records the appeal on your case.

## If the appeal fails

You can ask the care team about other options, including a complaint to the insurer or the regulator. Your follow-up care should not stop while a claim is disputed.
