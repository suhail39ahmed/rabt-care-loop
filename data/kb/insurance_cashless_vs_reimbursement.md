# Demo Health Insurance: cashless network or reimbursement

## About this plan

Demo Health Insurance is a fictional insurer used in this prototype. Real plans differ; check your own policy.

## Cashless (in-network)

At a clinic in the Demo Health Insurance network, you show your insurance card, pay only your co-pay, and the insurer pays the clinic directly. Rabt shows network clinics first.

## Reimbursement (pay and claim)

At a clinic outside the network, if your plan allows it, you pay the full bill yourself and then claim the money back. Reimbursement is usually limited to the network rate, so you may get back less than you paid.

## Which should I choose?

Cashless is simpler and usually cheaper. Ask the care team for a cost estimate before booking outside the network.
