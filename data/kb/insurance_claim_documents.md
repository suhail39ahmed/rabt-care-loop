# Demo Health Insurance: documents for a claim

## Documents you need

For a reimbursement claim keep:

- the itemised invoice (not only the card receipt);
- the payment receipt;
- the referral letter;
- the medical report or test result;
- your insurance card and Emirates ID copy.

## Time limit

Submit the claim within your plan's claim window. Late claims may be refused.

## Tracking your claim

Rabt shows your claim status: documents ready, submitted, approved or rejected, and paid. Keep copies of everything you send.
