# Demo Health Insurance: co-pay and deductibles

## What a co-pay is

A co-pay is the part of the bill you pay yourself at the clinic. On the fictional Demo Health Insurance plan it is a percentage of the visit cost, with a fixed upper limit per visit.

## What changes the amount

- Network clinics usually mean a lower co-pay than reimbursement claims.
- Preventive screening may have no co-pay; follow-up tests often do.
- Medicines and extra tests can have their own co-pay.

## Before you go

Ask the clinic or the care team for an estimate. If cost is a worry, tell us; there may be a closer or cheaper in-network option.
