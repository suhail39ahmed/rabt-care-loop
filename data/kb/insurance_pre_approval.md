# Demo Health Insurance: pre-approval

## What pre-approval is

Some tests and procedures, such as a colonoscopy, a breast ultrasound or a biopsy, need the insurer to agree before they are done. This is called pre-approval or prior authorisation.

## Who requests it

The clinic usually sends the request to Demo Health Insurance with your referral and the doctor's notes. Rabt tracks the request and tells you when a decision arrives. A simple request is often decided within a few working days.

## If pre-approval is refused

Ask the clinic for the reason. The doctor can send more information, or you can appeal. Do not cancel your follow-up without talking to the care team first.
