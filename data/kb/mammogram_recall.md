# Called back after a mammogram

## Why people are called back

Being called back after a screening mammogram is common. Most recalls happen because the radiologist wants clearer pictures of one area, and most people who are called back do not have cancer.

## What happens at the recall visit

You may have extra mammogram views or an ultrasound. Sometimes a small needle sample (biopsy) is recommended. You will be told what each test is for before it is done.

## Getting support

It is normal to feel anxious while waiting. You can bring someone with you and ask for a female clinician. Ask Rabt for a call from the care team if you want to talk it through.
