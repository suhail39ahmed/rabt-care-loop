# Understanding your mammogram result

## How results are reported

Mammogram reports often use a category scale (BI-RADS) from 0 to 6. In simple terms:

- **Category 1 or 2:** normal or a clearly harmless finding. Routine screening continues.
- **Category 0:** the pictures are incomplete and more images are needed. This is common and is not a diagnosis.
- **Category 3:** probably harmless; a shorter-interval check is usually advised.
- **Category 4 or 5:** an area needs a closer look, often with a biopsy.

## Dense breasts

A report may say your breasts are dense. This is common and normal, but it can make mammograms harder to read. Your clinician will tell you if extra imaging is useful.
