# Your rights as a patient

## You have the right to

- be treated with respect, whatever your nationality, religion, gender or ability to pay;
- receive information in a language you understand, with an interpreter if needed;
- ask questions and get a second opinion;
- ask for a chaperone or a clinician of the same gender for intimate examinations;
- refuse or delay a test after it has been explained to you.

## Raising a concern

Tell the care team first. If you are not satisfied, ask for the clinic's patient relations office. Rabt records concerns on your case so they are followed up.
