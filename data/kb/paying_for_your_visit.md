# Paying for your follow-up visit — approved FAQ

**Cashless (in-network):** if the clinic is in your insurer's network, you usually pay only your co-pay at the clinic and the insurer pays the rest directly.

**Pay and claim (reimbursement):** some plans let you pay the clinic yourself and claim the money back. Keep the itemised invoice, the payment receipt, the referral letter and the medical report. Submit the claim within your plan's claim window. Rabt shows your claim status: documents ready, submitted, approved or rejected, and paid.

**Self-pay:** an out-of-network clinic is only shown if your plan reimburses it or you choose self-pay.

Ask the care team for a cost estimate before you book. Your insurer makes the final decision on any claim.
