# Privacy and consent in Rabt

## What we use

Rabt uses the minimum information needed to arrange your follow-up: your screening result, contact details, insurance plan and preferences.

## How your data is protected

- Names, phone numbers and ID numbers are removed before any text is sent to an AI model.
- Only your care team and the clinic you book with can see your case.
- Every action on your case is logged.

## Consent

You agree to reminders when you join the programme. You can change how we contact you, or stop reminders, at any time; your doctor will still be told about an abnormal result. Ask the care team for a copy of the information we hold about you.
