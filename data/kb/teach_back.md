# Check you have understood (teach-back)

## What teach-back is

After we explain something, we may ask you to say it back in your own words. This is not a test of you; it checks that we explained it clearly.

## Example

"Can you tell me what you will do the day before your colonoscopy?" A good answer: "Only clear drinks, take the prep in two parts, and arrange someone to drive me home."

## If something is unclear

Say so. We will explain it again in another way, in your language, or arrange a call with a nurse.
